About Provocateur

We specialize in prescription lenses for smart glasses, with non-prescription options for anyone who wants a different lens without vision correction.

Our Mission

Smart glasses are changing the way we interact with the world. But for millions of people who wear prescription glasses, using them comfortably has been a challenge — until now.

At Provocateur, we craft custom replacement lenses specifically designed for Ray-Ban Meta, Meta Adventurer, Meta Fury, Oakley Meta HSTN, and Meta Starfire Kylie Edition AI Glasses, preserving every feature of your frames while giving you the crystal-clear vision you need.

Prescription wearers are at the heart of what we do. We also make frame-specific clear, photochromic, tinted, polarised, and blue-light replacement lenses for customers who do not need vision correction.

Every lens is crafted with Ultra High Index materials, shipped with premium coatings at no extra cost, and backed by our commitment to precision and quality.
